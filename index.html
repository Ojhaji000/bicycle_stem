Custom-designed Gearbox Prototyping

My Role: Design, Manufacturing, and Testing Engineer

The Challenge

A high-torque, low-RPM gearbox was required for a custom lifting mechanism. Due to size constraints and unique input/output shaft arrangements, an off-the-shelf solution was not feasible, requiring a ground-up design and fabrication.

The Prototyping Process

Key Fabrication Steps

Step 1: CAD & Gear Generation. Gears were custom-modeled using an involute gear generator in CAD software to meet the required module and pressure angle for a 10:1 gear ratio.
Step 2: Housing Fabrication. The gearbox housing was **CNC machined from Aluminum 6061** for its high strength-to-weight ratio and excellent machinability.
Step 3: Component Sourcing & Assembly. Sourced standard bearings and fasteners. The gears and shafts were press-fit into the housing and secured.
Step 4: Load Testing. The final assembly was mounted to a test bench and tested on a dynamometer to verify it met the $\text{100 N}\cdot\text{m}$ torque requirement under continuous operation.
